# Security Policy

Reporting a Vulnerability
We value the work of security researchers in keeping our community safe.
To report a vulnerability, please follow the protocol below:

1. How to Report
Please do not open a public issue for security vulnerabilities.

Reports should be sent via email to abdallah.ewina@gmail.com .

Please include the following in your report:

A clear description of the vulnerability.

The affected version(s).

Step-by-step instructions to reproduce the issue (PoC code or screenshots).

Potential impact if exploited.

2. What to Expect
Acknowledgment: We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours.

Triage: Our team will investigate the issue and may reach out for more details.
We aim to provide a status update every 7 days during the investigation.

Resolution: If the vulnerability is confirmed, we will work on a patch.
We ask that you do not disclose the issue publicly until a fix has been released.

Credit: Once the issue is resolved, we are happy to provide public credit for your discovery in our release notes,
unless you choose to remain anonymous.

3. Policy Scope
This policy applies only to the source code and digital assets within this repository.
Please act in good faith to avoid privacy violations,
 destruction of data, or interruption of our services.
